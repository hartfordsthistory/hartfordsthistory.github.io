---
title: José de Jesús Noé
---

1805 – 17 March 1862

José de Jesús Noé was a Californio, and the last Hispanic Alcalde (roughly equivalent to a mayor and a magistrate or sheriff) of Yerba Buena.

Californios were the the civilian settlers and colonists who emigrated into Alta California under the auspices of a faltering imperial Spain. They only gained independence from Spain after the _sixteen year long_ Mexican War of Independence which ended in fall of 1821. The secularization of the missions in 1833 opened up vast land grants to Mexican citizens, and the San Francisco Peninsula was divided up into several large ranchos.

One of them was Noé's Rancho San Miguel.



Noe Valley named for him.
